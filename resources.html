Posters

We have created a series of posters that are free to download and hang in your workplace. They are intended to raise awareness of the benefits to companies of people with high functioning Asperger's as well as awareness of Asperger's within the workforce. Just click on any of the posters below to download a high resolution PDF or download the whole set in our poster pack. We would appreciate it if you would let us know where you hang the posters so that we are able to follow up if necessary. We also provide business training and consultancy for forward thinking companies. If you'd like more information on these services, please get in touch.
